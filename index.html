    Practice mode

    Learn each kanji with its stroke order. You see the meaning and readings, and the kanji is drawn in grey for you to trace, with numbers showing the stroke order.

    • Auto hints (default): each kanji starts at tracing. Get it right (recognized, perfect stroke order, 70%+ accuracy) and the next time some strokes are hidden, then most, then all. A poor attempt brings hints back.
    • Or choose a fixed level: Trace, Partial or None.

    Switch to Test when you're ready to write from a meaning or reading alone.

    Test mode

    You'll get an English meaning, an on'yomi or a kun'yomi. Write the kanji in the box, then press Check.

    Kun'yomi are shown as stems, as in the PDF (e.g. かぞ for 数える). When several kanji share a reading, any of them is accepted.

    Skill (top bar) chooses what to practise: Write the kanji, recognise its Meaning, or know its Reading. Mixed rotates all three and favours the ones you find hardest. In Practice you pick from options and can tap 🔊 to hear readings. In Test you type a reading (romaji like kazu, or kana from a Japanese keyboard) or tap 🎤 and say it.

    Each writing attempt is graded on:

    • Recognized: does your drawing look more like the right kanji than any other kanji in the app?
    • Stroke order: each stroke is matched to a reference stroke, and checked for order and direction.
    • Accuracy: how closely the shape of your strokes matches the reference (0–100).

    Each answer shows the kanji's radical (blue strokes) and its parts; hover over one to see its strokes. In Test, the Hint button gives the radical first.

    Use the set button at the top to choose kanji by school grade, JLPT level, frequency or radical, and how to sort them. Click a kanji in Progress to see your stats for it. Kanji you struggle with come up more often. Progress is saved in this browser; use Progress → Export to move it to another device.

    Keys: Enter check / next · Ctrl+Z undo · Esc clear · R try again (practice)
